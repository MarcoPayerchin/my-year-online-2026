About this project

The wall is just the background. The pot with the plant stands for my online year 2026.

The plant hangs down along the wall. Every row is one day and every colour is a platform. The more squares, the longer I was there.

Hover over a day to see what happened on that day. While you scroll down, the sky turns into earth and then into the sea floor.

About this project

This is a semester project for the course Information Design. It started with a question I asked myself: what does my year online actually look like? I took my own data from Instagram, TikTok, Spotify and Safari and let it grow into a hanging plant. It climbs down a wall, one row per day, from January to October 2026.

How to read the plant

InstagramTikTokSpotifySafari

Every row is one day and every colour is a platform. One square is 30 minutes and half a square is 15. A blossom at the end of a row marks an extra long day with more than 10.5 hours.

Explore the months

Each month is its own page. Hover over a day and three signs on the wall show the life phase, the hours per platform and details like genre, search topic and time of day. The plant then splits into one column per platform, so you can compare the whole month.

Dive down

Keep scrolling and the sky turns into earth, then water, then the sea floor. Down there you first guess my total time online, then find out how close you were and which patterns connect my phases of life.

Where the data comes from

Exports from Instagram, TikTok, Spotify and Safari, from all of my devices. The hours for Instagram, TikTok and Safari are estimated from timestamps and calibrated with my screen time. Spotify also counts music in the background. The year is not over yet, so the plant ends in October.
